`lighterDepositFlow` returns `venueDepositPipeline` for an existing Lighter Robinhood account. The venue `DEPOSIT` action then credits the USDG on Robinhood Chain to the account, and a USDG source needs no swap. The `DepositFlowVenueDepositPipeline` TSDoc describes this case.
