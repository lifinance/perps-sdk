`Provider.minDepositUsd`, `minOrderValueUsd`, `minWithdrawalUsd`, `depositFeeUsd` and `withdrawalFeeUsd` are now `DecimalString` instead of `number`. The venue-wide `Provider.minOrderValueUsd` and the per-market `PerpsMarket.minOrderValueUsd` therefore carry one type, and a caller compares them without a parse step. Hyperliquid `getWithdrawableBalances` reads the descriptor fee as a decimal string.
