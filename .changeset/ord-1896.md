Spot rows from `getAccount` now carry `transferable`: the part of `units` that the venue releases from that category. Hyperliquid sets it to `total` minus `hold`, and Lighter sets it to `balance` minus `locked_balance`, clamped to `[0, units]`. The `Balance.transferable` doc states that every collateral row and every spot row carries it.
