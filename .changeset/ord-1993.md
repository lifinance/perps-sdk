`PerpsClient.getAccount`, `getMarketSettings`, `accountExists`, `getDepositFlow` and `getWithdrawFlow` accept an optional `SDKRequestOptions` argument and send it to the provider plugin read.

BREAKING: `SDKRequestOptions.lighterAuthToken` is removed. `SDKRequestOptions` has only `signal`. The Lighter provider resolves the auth token from the constructor `authToken` option or from the storage adapter, and throws `SetupRequired` when no source gives a token.
