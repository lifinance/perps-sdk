Fix `mapOrderStatus` misclassifying documented Hyperliquid terminal statuses (e.g. `siblingFilledCanceled`, `scheduledCancel`, `liquidatedCanceled`, and the `*Rejected` family) as PENDING, which kept cancelled/rejected orders in the live open-orders and trigger-orders lists instead of evicting them.
