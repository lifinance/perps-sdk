`getMarketSettings` is now a required provider method and `PerpsClient.getMarketSettings` returns `Promise<MarketSettings>`: it resolves the venue leverage and margin mode for a perps market (Lighter falls back to the market default initial margin fraction with cross margin, Ondo reads its leverage endpoint) and throws a `PerpsError` for a spot market, a failed read, or a response with no usable value.
