Harden the Lighter auth-token lifecycle: a failed read-only token creation now retries with bounded backoff instead of downgrading reads to the write-capable standard token for the whole session, the requested read-only expiry keeps a clock-skew margin under Lighter's 10-year cap, and a server-side-revoked standard token is re-signed on retry instead of failing reads until it expires.
