Lighter `getAccount` spot balance rows now carry a `price` and a USD value from the spot market's mark price, and `getAccountSummary` adds every spot row, the settlement row included, to `portfolioValue`. `getAccount` now also reads the backend `/marketsContext` and rejects when that read fails. The `LighterAccountConfig.totalAssetValue` doc now states that the figure is perps-route equity and excludes the spot-route balances.
