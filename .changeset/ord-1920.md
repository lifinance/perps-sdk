Authenticated per-user reads now throw `PerpsError` with `PerpsErrorCode.Unauthorized` when the account has no credential, instead of returning an empty result.

- Lighter: `getOrders`, `getOrder`, `getFills`, `getPortfolioHistory` and `getActivity` throw when no auth token resolves. `getOrder` previously threw `SDKError`.
- Ondo: `getWithdrawableBalances`, `getAvailableToTrade`, `getPositions`, `getOrders`, `getOrder`, `getFills`, `getActivity` and `getPortfolioHistory` throw when no session token is stored, and when the venue rejects the stored token. `getOrder` previously threw `SDKError`.
- Unchanged: Lighter `getAccount`, and Ondo `getAccount`, `accountExists`, `getDepositFlow` and `getWithdrawFlow` keep their logged-out answers. Hyperliquid is unchanged.
- `@lifi/perps-sdk`: TSDoc on the affected `PerpsClient` reads and services documents the `Unauthorized` throw.
