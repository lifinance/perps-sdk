Model the venue transfer-margin requirement as provider knowledge. Every provider plugin now implements `removableMargin(position)`, returning the margin the venue will let a client move out of an isolated position, or `undefined` where the venue has no per-position margin transfer. Hyperliquid maps its documented `max(initial_margin_required, 0.1 * total_position_value)`; Lighter maps the initial-margin requirement alone (it publishes no notional floor); Ondo declares the rule absent, being cross-margined only. The shared `removableIsolatedMargin` helper composes the rule in exact decimal arithmetic, taking the initial-margin term at the position's own leverage and rounding so the figure can only under-report.

The `removableMargin` export from `@lifi/perps-sdk` is removed: it modelled a leverage-cap ceiling at the market's `maxLeverage`, which no venue enforces on a margin transfer, and over-reported the removable amount on any position above `1 / notionalFloorRatio` leverage. Resolve the market's provider and call `provider.removableMargin(position)` instead.
