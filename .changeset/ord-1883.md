`getWithdrawableBalances` rows with a `withdrawalFee` can carry `isFeeDeducted`: `true` when the venue takes the fee out of the requested amount (Hyperliquid), `false` when the venue charges it in addition to the requested amount (Ondo). An absent `isFeeDeducted` means unknown. The client drops it from a row without a fee.
