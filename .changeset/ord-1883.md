`getWithdrawableBalances` rows with a `withdrawalFee` can carry `withdrawalFeeMode`: `'deducted'` when the venue takes the fee out of the requested amount (Hyperliquid), or `'onTop'` when the venue charges it in addition to the requested amount (Ondo). An absent `withdrawalFeeMode` means that the mode is unknown. The client drops the mode from a row without a fee and rejects a mode outside `'deducted' | 'onTop'`.
