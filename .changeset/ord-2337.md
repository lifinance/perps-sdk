If a caller aborts a request with a custom reason or with `AbortSignal.timeout()`, the request rejects with `signal.reason`, not with `PerpsError(ServerError)`. For this cancel, `fetchWithRetry` does not call `shouldRetry` and does not wait for a retry delay. The new `isAbortRejection(error, signal)` export identifies a cancel for a provider transport.
