`LtLiquidation` now declares the `trade` and `info` payload that Lighter's `/api/v1/liquidations` returns, and types `type` as the documented `'partial' | 'deleverage'` union instead of `string`. The Lighter liquidation activity therefore reports `liquidatedNotionalPosition` (the forced trade's price times its size), `accountValue` (the pre-trade account value, present only when Lighter reports the cross risk snapshot), `leverageType` (`cross` or `isolated`, read from the row's own position margin mode; an unknown mode reports no `leverageType`), and the liquidated position `size`. A consumer that constructs an `LtLiquidation` must supply the two new members.
