Skip Hyperliquid HIP-4 outcome market identities in the WebSocket and REST mappers. The provider no longer emits a market context, a position, an order, a fill, or a spot balance for a coin that starts with `#`, a token name that starts with `+`, or an asset ID at or above 100000000.

The `./utils` subpath adds the `assetIsOutcome` predicate.

A provider with an attached market registry no longer emits a `marketsContext` entry for a market id that the registry does not list. A provider with no registry keeps the previous fast-feed fallback.
