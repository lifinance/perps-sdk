Skip Hyperliquid HIP-4 outcome market identities in the WebSocket and REST mappers. The provider no longer emits a market context, a position, an order, a fill, or a spot balance for a coin that starts with `#`, a token name that starts with `+`, or an asset ID at or above 100000000. The WebSocket market context also holds back a market id that the attached market registry does not list.
