The Lighter provider declares every account, position, and trade wire field that Lighter marks `omitempty` as an optional member. Each mapper supplies an explicit default for such a field. A position with no accrued funding, a Classic/Simple account, and a flat counterparty each produced `undefined` against a required contract field. The provider now emits the zero value instead.

The package also exports `LT_ACCOUNT_TRADING_MODE_SIMPLE` and `LT_ACCOUNT_TRADING_MODE_UNIFIED`, the two Lighter account-trading-mode wire values, beside the existing `LT_MARGIN_MODE_*` pair.
