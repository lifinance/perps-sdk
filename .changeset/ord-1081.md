Expose `positionSupportsMarginAdjustment(position)` and `positionSupportsMarginRemoval(position)` as the stack's owned answer to whether an open position takes a margin adjustment, and in which direction. Clients gating an edit-margin affordance read these instead of inspecting `Position.marginMode` and `Position.market.positionMarginAdjustment` themselves, or calling `positionMarginConstraints` just to test its `undefined` return. `removableIsolatedMargin` and the Hyperliquid and Lighter `positionMarginConstraints` implementations now gate on the same predicates, so a client's affordance cannot diverge from what the venue accepts.
