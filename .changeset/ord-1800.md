The Lighter `getAccount` now sums `marginUsed` and `unrealizedPnl` as exact decimals, so the account totals show no float drift. A position whose `marginUsed` or `unrealizedPnl` is not a decimal now makes `getAccount` reject with a `PerpsError` (`SDKError`) that names the field, in place of a `'NaN'` total. The WebSocket `accountSummary` channel now renders its four values as plain decimals, never in exponent notation.
