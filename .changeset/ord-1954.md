Report a reconnect replay send that throws `SetupRequired` or `Unauthorized` through the subscription `onError` callback, remove that wire from replay, and keep other accounts and public channels active. `WsProviderBase` exposes the protected `resolveChannelKeys` hook, which the Lighter and Ondo providers override. The Ondo provider cycles the connection on the last private release only when it sent a login on that connection.
