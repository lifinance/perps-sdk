`PerpsSigner` now has exactly two values: `USER` (the end-user's wallet must sign or consent — expect a wallet interaction) and `SDK` (the provider package completes signing with credentials it holds or creates, no user interaction). The previous `AGENT` and `API_KEY` values are removed; each provider package is the authority on what `SDK` means for its venue. The array shape of `signers` is unchanged.
