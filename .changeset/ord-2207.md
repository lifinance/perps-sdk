`LiquidationEstimateParams` has a required `marginMode`, an optional `size`, and an optional `position`. `estimateLiquidationPrice` takes an optional `size` and `current` position, and blends the order into that position. `estimateLiquidationPriceAtMarketRate`, which is the Lighter and Ondo `estimateLiquidationPrice`, gives `undefined` for a cross order or a cross position, and for an order against the side of the position. For an isolated add, it blends the order into the open position, with `position.marginUsed` as the collateral.
