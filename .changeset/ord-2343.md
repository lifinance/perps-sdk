The Ondo `marketsContext` and `marketContext` streams subscribe only to `markPricesPerps` and `fundingRatesPerps`. The Ondo contexts have no `midPrice` property. To get the best bid and the best ask, subscribe to the `orderbook` channel. The `OndoWsMessage` type has no `topOfBooksPerps` member.
