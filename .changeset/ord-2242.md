Add a shared spot category id and an order direction label.

- `@lifi/perps-types` exports `SPOT_CATEGORY_ID`.
- `@lifi/perps-sdk` exports `isSpotCategory(categoryId)` and `orderSideLabel(side, market)`. `orderSideLabel` returns `'Buy'` or `'Sell'` on a spot market and `'Long'` or `'Short'` on a perp market.
- The Hyperliquid `SPOT_MARKET_ID` and the Lighter `LIGHTER_SPOT_CATEGORY_ID` are now aliases of `SPOT_CATEGORY_ID`. Their value does not change.
