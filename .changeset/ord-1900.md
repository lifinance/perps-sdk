Add `InternalTransferActivity` and `InternalTransferBalance` to the shared activity contract. Hyperliquid staking, borrow-lend, and spot/perps movements return `INTERNAL_TRANSFER` with source and destination balances, not a counterparty. Include `INTERNAL_TRANSFER` in activity filters and handle the new union member. `TRANSFER` retains its counterparty requirement and returns genuine transfers only. Venue amounts and borrow-lend metadata remain unchanged.
