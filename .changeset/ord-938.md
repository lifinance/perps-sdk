Add an optional `Provider.depositAsset` (`DepositAsset`) describing the on-chain ERC-20 collateral token a client bridges in to fund an account — carrying `chainId`, `address`, `decimals` plus `displaySymbol`/`logoURI` for display. Distinct from a category's `quoteAsset` (the pricing unit); additive and optional, so existing `/providers` payloads and consumers are unaffected.
