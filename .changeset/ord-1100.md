Remove the dead `Provider.depositAssets` field and the `DepositAsset` interface. Nothing populated the field — the `/providers` projection that fed it was withdrawn — so any consumer reading it received `undefined`. Per-venue deposit facts are declared in `@lifi/perps-sdk` as `DeclaredDepositAsset` constants (`ETHEREUM_USDC`, `HYPERLIQUID_USDC`, `LIGHTER_USDC`, …) and resolved at runtime through `getDepositFlow`; read deposit targets from there instead.
