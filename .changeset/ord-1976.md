`HlWsFastAssetCtx.markPx` and `HlWsFastAssetCtx.midPx` are now `string | number`, because Hyperliquid documents them as numbers. The WebSocket provider and `mapMarketContext` convert a numeric fast-feed price to a plain-notation decimal string, and ignore a fast-feed price that is not a string or a number.
