The `marketsContext` channel delivers a maximum of one event each `WS_MARKETS_CONTEXT_INTERVAL_MS` (1,000 ms) for each provider. The first event after the channel opens is immediate. Wire frames inside the gap coalesce, and the newest context map is delivered when the gap ends. `WsProviderBase.emitMarketsContext` holds the one implementation. The `marketContext` channel and all other channels have no limit.
