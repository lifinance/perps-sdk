Add an optional subscription-error callback. Report venue authentication rejection without a change to socket status, stop rejected private feeds, and resolve current credentials for explicit subscriptions after recovery. `WsProviderBase` exposes `rejectChannel` and `wireSubs` to subclasses. The Ondo provider waits for the venue login acknowledgement and reports a missing acknowledgement as `TimeoutError`.
