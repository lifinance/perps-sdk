Send the exact native Lighter IMF on leverage actions and use it for order margin. `UpdateLeverageParams` and `PlaceOrderParams` accept an optional `initialMarginFractionBps`, the Lighter provider attaches the IMF to each `createAction` leverage request (a present value unchanged, else the half-up encoding of the leverage), `isLeverageEqual` compares a requested native IMF, `calculateOrderAmounts` takes the IMF as its exact margin rate, and `resolveExactLeverage` gives the formula leverage of a `MarketSettings` or `Position`. `resolveActionRequest` receives the caller's params.
