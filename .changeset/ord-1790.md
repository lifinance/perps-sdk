The Hyperliquid `orderUpdates` stream now sets `explorerLink` on each order when the explorer holds the placement transaction, so a stream upsert keeps the link that `getOrders` returns. A stream frame waits at most 3 seconds for the explorer read and emits its orders unlinked when the read times out or fails.
