**Breaking:** `OpenOrder.size` is removed. `OpenOrder` now carries `originalSize` (the quantity the order was submitted for) and `remainingSize` (the quantity still resting on the book), matching the names `Order` already uses. The old `size` field held the remaining quantity on Hyperliquid and the original quantity on Lighter, so `filledSize / size` returned a wrong fill fraction on one of the two. Replace a read of `size` with `remainingSize` for the resting quantity, or with `originalSize` for the submitted quantity. `expectedRealizedPnlForOpenOrder` now projects `remainingSize`, which corrects its result for a partially filled Lighter order.
