Resolve the Lighter `marketContext` stats channel from the market's category instead of its numeric market id, so a market in Lighter's new id range reaches the correct channel. The subscription now syncs the market registry first and throws `MarketNotFound` when the backend market list does not carry the market id, or when the provider was constructed without a `PerpsSDKClient`.
