Sign Lighter withdrawals, transfers and spot/perp route moves against the collateral asset of the instance doing the signing instead of a module-pinned USDC index, and report collateral balances per instance. `LighterInstanceConfig` now carries a `collateral` field (L2 asset index plus display symbol — USDC on mainnet, USDG on the Robinhood chain deployment), `LighterSigner` accepts a `collateralAssetIndex`, and `lighterAsset` accepts the owning instance's provider key.
