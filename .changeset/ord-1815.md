The Lighter `getAccount` perps `collateralBalances` rows now report the perps-route holding: one row for each asset with a `margin_balance` above zero, priced like the spot rows. An account whose open positions use all the free margin now shows its collateral. For buying power, read `AccountSummary.availableMargin`.
