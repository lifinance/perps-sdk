SDK-owned per-leg wallet chain-switching now extends to `SigningMethod.EVM_TX` actions. `SignActionsContext` gains an optional bound `switchToChain(chainId)` that core populates from the consumer's `switchChain` hook, and Lighter's `EVM_TX` signer (deposit/approve/withdraw) switches the wallet to each leg's `txParams.chainId` before broadcasting. When no `switchChain` hook is configured (local/private-key signer) it retains the fail-loud wrong-chain guard rather than broadcasting on the wrong chain.
