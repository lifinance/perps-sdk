The Lighter order mappers now compare Lighter's wire spellings directly against the adopted order enums. `mapOrderType`, `mapTimeInForce` and `mapOrderStatus` no longer normalize hyphens to underscores, and `mapOrderStatus` no longer accepts `in_progress`. **Breaking:** `mapStatusReason` takes `LtOrderStatusEnum` instead of `string`, so its 13 `canceled-*` literals fail to compile if Lighter drops one from the wire enum.
