The Lighter signer signs a grouped `PLACE_ORDER` step (an entry order with an attached TP/SL) with `_signCreateGroupedOrders`. The package exports the grouped step contract: `LtCreateGroupedOrdersWasmParams`, the `LT_GROUPING_TYPE_*` and `LT_TX_TYPE_CREATE_GROUPED_ORDERS` constants, `isLtGroupedOrderStep`, and `resolveLtTxType`.
