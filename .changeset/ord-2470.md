Add the opt-in `bounds` to `getPortfolioHistory`: an inclusive `start` and `end` in Unix milliseconds. A bounded read returns the latest valid sample at or before `start` as `PortfolioHistoryResponse.opening`, when the venue has one, and only the samples after `start` through `end` as `points`. `pnl` and `netTransfers` are relative to the first returned sample, and an absent `opening` never means a zero balance. Lighter and Lighter RH read `/api/v1/pnl` from one bucket before `start` through `end`, at the earliest from 0, with `count_back` plus one for the finite ranges. Hyperliquid and Ondo take the opening from the native preset, or from the narrowest wider preset of the same scope that has one. A wider preset the SDK cannot join or read is skipped with a warning. `@lifi/perps-types` adds `PortfolioHistoryBounds`, and `@lifi/perps-sdk` exports `buildPortfolioHistoryWindow`. A request without `bounds` keeps its behavior.
